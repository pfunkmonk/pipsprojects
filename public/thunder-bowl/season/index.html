First-time setup: connect all four signed-in sources once

The helper reads only the required weekly component-stat tables after you press Update everything. It uses your normal signed-in CBS, Footballguys, FantasyPros, and PFF sessions and never receives or stores any password.

  1. Download and unzip the helper.
  2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome or edge://extensions in Edge, turn on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, and select the unzipped folder.
  3. Sign into CBS, Footballguys, FantasyPros, and PFF in the same browser profile, then reload this page.
