First-time setup: connect your CBS login once

The helper reads the 12-team Thunder Bowl roster report and CBS weekly component-stat projection tables only when you press Update everything. It uses your normal signed-in CBS tab and never receives or stores your password.

  1. Download and unzip the helper.
  2. Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, and select the unzipped folder.
  3. Sign into Thunder Bowl on CBS in the same Chrome profile. That is the only sign-in setup.
